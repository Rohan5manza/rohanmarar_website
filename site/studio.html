Content Studio.

Update your profile, projects, writing, books, experience, and Other entries without touching the design.

How updates work
1. Edit the fields below. Your draft stays in this browser.
2. Preview the draft, then export content.json.
3. Replace site/content.json in the source and redeploy, then commit it to GitHub.
This editor does not publish changes to the live portfolio. Keep a copy of your export; browser drafts are not a backup.
